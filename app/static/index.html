Tab accepts the first suggestion, Alt+1/2/3 the first, second or third; clicking a chip works too. Accepting inserts the word and a space (one key press). After a space the model predicts the next word; in the middle of a word it completes it. The context is the current sentence only (it restarts after . ! ?).

Keystroke savings = 1 − keys pressed / characters produced (backspaces count as keys; the counter restarts when the box is emptied; text loaded by “Try a prefix” or pasted is not counted).
