Cross-domain: Shakespeare vs modern English

Does autocomplete trained on modern English work on Shakespeare, and vice versa? The WikiText-2 training text was subsampled to the same number of tokens as the Shakespeare training text, so the comparison is about domain, not data size. Every model is scored on both test sets against the real target words (a target that is missing from the model's vocabulary counts as a miss).
