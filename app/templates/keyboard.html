Keyboard

Type with the physical keyboard or tap the on-screen keys. After a space the model predicts the next word; in the middle of a word it completes it. The context is the current sentence only (it restarts after . ! ?), exactly as in the evaluation.

Shortcuts: Tab accepts the first suggestion, Alt+1/2/3 the first, second or third; clicking a chip (or a bar in the inspector) works too. Accepting inserts the word and a space and counts as one key press; a following . , ; : ! ? removes that space. Keystroke savings = 1 − keys pressed / characters produced (backspaces count as keys; the counter restarts when the box is emptied; text loaded by "Try a prefix" or pasted is not counted).

Prediction inspector

The ten most probable words for the current context and model (after filtering and the prefix filter, probabilities renormalised over the candidate words). The three highlighted bars are the suggestion chips. Click a bar to accept that word.
